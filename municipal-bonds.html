Overview

Bond-level data on U.S. municipal bonds outstanding as of 1929.

Coverage includes every large city (population of 100,000 or more in 1930) and smaller cities in California, Indiana, Massachusetts, New York and Ohio.

Funding: Economic History Association.

Sources

  • Moody's Manual of Governments, 1929 edition.
