Overview

A city-level panel of municipal revenue, expenditure and debt, reported annually from 1924 to 1938.

Variables are aggregated to comparable categories across sources. A separate file documents how the categories line up across the underlying publications.

Funding: Economic History Association.

Sources

  • U.S. Census Bureau, Financial Statistics of Cities (annual).
  • State archival records of municipal finances.

Coverage notes

Disaggregated data, especially for Massachusetts and New York, are available from the author on request.
