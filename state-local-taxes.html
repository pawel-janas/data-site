Overview

Annual state and local tax rates for personal income, corporate income, property, sales and excise taxes. For each tax type, the series extends back as far as the sources allow.

Local tax rates are aggregated to the county, which gives a stable, non-overlapping geography that maps well to other measures of government and economic activity.

Sources

  • NBER TAXSIM; Tax Foundation; CCH CorpSystem.
  • State and local tax authorities (typically departments of revenue), and data from prior research.
