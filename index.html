Data

Datasets on U.S. cities, counties, and state and local public finance

Panel and cross-sectional datasets transcribed from historical government publications, financial manuals and state archives, together with a modern panel of state and local tax rates. Each dataset is archived on openICPSR with a permanent DOI; this site documents the coverage, sources and files and gives the citation for each.

Compiled by Pawel Janas, California Institute of Technology

Citing the data

Please cite the paper associated with a dataset, if any, and the openICPSR project citation. Each dataset page gives both, with BibTeX.

Files are distributed through openICPSR, which asks users to sign in (free) before downloading. Several of the historical datasets were funded by the Economic History Association.
